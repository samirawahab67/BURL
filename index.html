Every Grain Tells a Story.

Rooted in the natural beauty of burl wood, BURL transforms distinctive grain patterns and organic forms into contemporary furniture. Each piece celebrates the character of its material, bringing together craftsmanship, sculptural design, and timeless elegance.

Material & craft

Nature, in Its Most Distinctive Form.

Burl is the rarest cut in the tree. We use it the way a jeweller uses a stone.

A burl forms where a tree grows around stress: a wound, an insect, a cluster of buds that never opened. The grain folds back on itself in eyes and swirls instead of running straight. Slice it into veneer and every leaf is different from the last, so no BURL piece can repeat another.

  1. 01

    Choosing the flitch

    We select each bundle of burl veneer by hand and log its number against the piece it will become.

  2. 02

    Book-matching

    Consecutive leaves are opened like the pages of a book so the figure mirrors across the joint.

  3. 03

    Shaping the form

    Curved cores are laminated in moulds, then veneered so the grain wraps each radius without a break.

  4. 04

    Hand finishing

    Twelve coats of lacquer, rubbed back between each, until the grain looks wet and deep.
